🔐 Protect Your Privacy

Your personal information should not be shared carelessly. Review your privacy settings regularly.

Important Privacy Habits

  • Use strong passwords: Avoid using easily guessed information.
  • Enable 2FA: Add an extra layer of account protection.
  • Review location sharing: Only share your location when necessary.
  • Protect personal information: Avoid sharing passwords, OTPs, addresses, or other sensitive details.
  • Check app permissions: Review which apps can access your information.
💡 Think before you share. Once personal information is shared online, controlling where it goes can become difficult.

🔗 Think Before You Click Challenge

Would you click an unexpected prize link? Check it before entering any personal information.

Imagine you receive this link while browsing online.

Congratulations! You have been selected for a special reward.

Click the link below to claim your prize:

https://claim-prize.example/winner

⚠️ This is a safe educational simulation. It does not collect real information.

⚠️ Suspicious Link Detected!

Before clicking an unexpected link, look for warning signs:

  • You did not enter any contest.
  • The link uses an unfamiliar domain.
  • It may try to collect personal information.
  • Never enter your password or OTP.
  • Never provide banking details through an unknown link.

🚫 Stop Cyberbullying

Cyberbullying can happen through social media, messaging apps, gaming platforms, and other digital spaces.

1

Don't Engage

Avoid escalating the situation by responding with more abuse.

2

Block

Use platform controls to stop unwanted contact.

3

Report

Report harmful behaviour through the platform.

4

Seek Help

Tell a trusted adult, teacher, parent, or appropriate support person.

🎯 Safe or Unsafe?

Someone is repeatedly sending abusive messages to your friend. You forward the messages to other students for entertainment.

📵 Practice Digital Detox

Focus Time
